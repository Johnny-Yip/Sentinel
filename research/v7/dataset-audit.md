Each fold accounts for all 1,243,178 inputs exactly once as an eligible role or
an exclusion. Fold input/exclusion counts overlap across experiments and must
not be summed as independent data. Detailed reasons, dates, per-source class
counts, fingerprints and checks are in the JSON.

All four folds pass structural coverage and pooled two-class checks. Every
development role includes each required source. JUnit4's zero-positive policy
role produces a per-repository warning in the other three folds. Its calibration
role has just 27 positive snapshots on three dates; per-repository calibration
or policy rules therefore need careful support requirements. No boundary was
adjusted to rescue this condition.

The candidate monthly test population is 25,845 rows across four monthly dates,
not V6's 12,920 quarter-end test rows. A future raw-versus-calibrated comparison
must use identical models and test identities within its own experiment; a
difference from the published V6 table would otherwise confound schedule/cadence.

## Dependence and interpretation

Adjacent same-file outcome windows overlap for 526,664 collections, 136,492 io,
270,749 lang and 302,724 junit4 pairs. This reflects monthly sampling and repeated
paths. These are diagnostics, not independent sample counts or estimated effective
sample sizes. Some paths have only one observation; repeated-file counts are
recorded separately in the JSON.

**Assessment:** candidate chronology is computationally feasible for pooled
source development, with class-support warnings. It is not statistically
certified, frozen or evaluated for V7 performance. Nested source-only calibration
family selection, temporal robustness windows and confirmation remain design
work. Dataset expansion is recommended for broader repository-level conclusions.
